# AI Disclosure

**Last reviewed:** {{product.effectiveDate}}

## What uses AI

{{organization.productName}} uses machine-learning models in the places marked
with an "AI" badge in the interface. Where you see that badge, the content was
generated by a model ({{ai.providerLabel}}) and not written by a person.

{{ai.disclosureText}}

## What this means in practice

- **Output can be wrong.** Models produce fluent text that is confidently
  incorrect. Numbers, names, citations, and legal or medical statements need
  independent verification.
- **Output is a draft.** {{ai.humanReview}}
- **No professional advice.** AI output is not legal, medical, financial, or
  tax advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional.

## Your data

- Training on your content: **{{ai.trainingOnUserData}}**.
- What we send to the model provider: only the text of the request you make.
  We do not send your password, your session identifiers, or your payment data.
- Our provider's policy: {{ai.providerUrl}}

## Limits we enforce

- Automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you are
  **not** made by AI alone. See the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) for your
  rights, including the right to human review.
- We do not use AI to generate content that deceives people about its origin,
  and we do not use likeness or voice cloning on real people.

## Reporting a problem

If AI output is wrong, harmful, or exposed something it should not have, email
{{organization.supportEmail}} with the text and roughly when it appeared. We log
prompts and outputs for {{retention.auditLogs}} days for exactly this purpose.
