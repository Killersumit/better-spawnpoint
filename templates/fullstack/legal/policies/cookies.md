# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** {{product.effectiveDate}}

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. We also use
`localStorage` for the same purposes; this policy covers both.

## The short version

Strictly necessary storage keeps you signed in and stops cross-site attacks.
Nothing else — no analytics, no marketing, no third-party trackers — loads
until you actively turn it on. Declining is as easy as accepting, and nothing
breaks if you decline.

## What we do not do

- We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles.
- We do not let third parties set cookies on our pages before you consent.
- We do not treat continued browsing as consent, and we do not use cookie walls.

## Changing your choices

Open **Cookie settings** in the footer, or clear site data in your browser. The
banner will appear again on your next visit. Withdrawing consent stops the
processing from that point on; it does not retroactively make earlier processing
unlawful.

## Your browser's own controls

- Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
- Safari: Settings → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking

Blocking strictly necessary storage will stop sign-in from working — that is the
browser, not a dark pattern.
